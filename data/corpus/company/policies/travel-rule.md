# Travel rule policy (synthetic)

For outbound and inbound transfers of $3,000 or more, the exchange collects and transmits originator and
beneficiary information to the counterparty VASP. Transfers to unhosted wallets above the threshold require the
customer to attest wallet ownership. Missing travel rule data places the transfer in the compliance review queue.
