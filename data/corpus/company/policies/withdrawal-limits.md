# Retail withdrawal limits policy (synthetic)

This policy defines withdrawal limits for verified retail customers of the exchange. It supersedes the
previous version dated 2025-11-01, which set the daily limit at $25,000.

## Daily limit
The daily withdrawal limit for verified retail customers is $50,000 across all assets, measured in USD at the
time of each withdrawal request. The limit resets at 00:00 UTC.

## Exceptions
Customers may request a temporary limit increase through Support. Increases above $100,000 require a second
review by Compliance and an updated source-of-funds check. Support agents must never promise an increase
before the review completes.

## Holds
New payment methods trigger a 72-hour withdrawal hold. Withdrawals to addresses added in the last 24 hours are
held for manual review when they exceed $10,000.
