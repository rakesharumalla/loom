# Incident severity levels (synthetic)

SEV1: customer funds at risk or trading halted for all users. Page the incident commander immediately.
SEV2: a core flow is degraded for a large share of customers, such as delayed withdrawals or deposits.
SEV3: a partial degradation with a workaround. SEV4: cosmetic issues.
Every SEV1 and SEV2 requires a status page update within 15 minutes and a post-incident review within 5 business days.
