# Escalation handbook (synthetic)

Support escalates to Compliance for suspected sanctions matches, account takeover with fund movement, and any
law enforcement request. Support escalates to Payments SRE for queue backlogs, stuck withdrawals, and failed
deposits that affect more than 50 customers. Use the #payments-oncall channel and include the incident ticket.
