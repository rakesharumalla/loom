# Support macro: delayed withdrawals (synthetic)

Acknowledge the delay, confirm the asset and amount, and check the status page. If a SEV2 for the withdrawal
queue is open, tell the customer their withdrawal is queued and funds are safe. Do not ask the customer to resubmit,
because resubmission creates duplicate requests. Contact care-leads@example.com if the customer is a VIP.
Never share internal queue depth numbers with customers.
